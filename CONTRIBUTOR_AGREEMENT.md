# OpenDKG Contributor Agreement

**Version:** 1.0 — proposed for adoption  
**Maintained at:** https://github.com/OpenDKG/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md  
**Project operator:** the individual volunteer maintainers collaborating through the OpenDKG GitHub organization; **OpenDKG is not represented by this document as a registered company or separate legal person**.

> **Adoption requirement.** Publishing this document is not agreement. Every founding maintainer and later contributor must affirmatively accept the exact version before the team treats it as binding on that person. The initial three maintainers must separately record unanimous adoption of the initial governance terms. Existing code is not retroactively licensed without permission from the relevant rightsholders. This is a draft for legal review in applicable jurisdictions, not a guarantee of enforceability or immunity from liability.

## 1. What repositories are covered

A **Covered Repository** is a repository *owned by the `OpenDKG` GitHub organization* when a contribution is intentionally submitted for inclusion, whether the repository exists now or is created in the future. This agreement is intended to govern contributions to **all current and future Covered Repositories** after a contributor's valid acceptance, without needing a new project-specific agreement for each new repository.

**Express exclusion: NetPetra.** The NetPetra software/project and any repository principally dedicated to it are excluded, even if later transferred to the OpenDKG organization. Inclusion would require a separate, explicit written decision by the maintainers and permission from affected contributors; it is **not** automatic.

The agreement does not govern repositories outside OpenDKG ownership, personal forks, outside employers' code, or material already licensed under other terms merely because it is subsequently copied or transferred into a Covered Repository. Transferring an existing repository to OpenDKG does not retroactively license its history. Each such repository requires an ownership, provenance and license review before inclusion.

## 2. Who is granting rights

The **Contributor** is the individual or rights-holder validly submitting the Contribution. Public GitHub pseudonyms are allowed. A username or signing key does not, by itself, prove civil identity or ownership of intellectual property.

The Contributor retains copyright in material they own. **No copyright assignment, transfer of trademarks, employment contract, or transfer of title to shared assets** is created by this agreement. Because OpenDKG is not a separate legal person, the Contributor grants the public permissions described below **directly to each lawful recipient of the contribution**, including current and future maintainers, redistributors and users.

## 3. Contributions and license grants

A **Contribution** is copyrightable code, tests, build scripts, documentation, design, or other material intentionally submitted for incorporation into a Covered Repository after acceptance, plus earlier material explicitly identified and authorized under section 7. Material clearly marked **"Not a Contribution"** at submission is excluded unless separately authorized. Discussion alone does not necessarily grant rights over independently owned material.

**Default license:** OpenDKG intends to release its original software under the **Apache License, Version 2.0 (SPDX `Apache-2.0`)**. A Covered Repository must carry its *own* appropriate `LICENSE` file. The `LICENSE` in `OpenDKG/.github` covers that repository's material only; GitHub does not automatically copy it into other repositories.

For a Contribution intentionally submitted to an Apache-2.0 Covered Repository, the Contributor, to the extent authorized, grants each recipient the copyright and applicable patent permissions under **Apache-2.0**, subject to its terms. The Apache-2.0 copyright grant is perpetual and irrevocable as described in that license; its patent grant has the stated exceptions, including the patent-litigation termination provision. The grant does not expire when the Contributor resigns, is removed, changes handle, or ceases work.

If a Covered Repository expressly uses another license, obtain the Contributor's **separate affirmative agreement for that repository and its stated license** before merging. This general agreement does not silently relicense a contribution under an unexpected future license, nor permit maintainers to relicense other people's contributions just by voting. For third-party materials, preserve original licenses and notices and obtain permissions as necessary; this agreement cannot grant rights the Contributor does not possess.

## 4. Contributor assurances

To the best of their knowledge after reasonable diligence, each Contributor confirms that:

1. They authored the material or have the rights and any employer, client or coauthor approval required to submit it under the applicable license.
2. They have not knowingly included proprietary, stolen, leaked, confidential, or unauthorized content.
3. They disclose third-party code, data, models, images, AI-assisted material and required licenses/attributions in the contribution description.
4. They will promptly notify maintainers if an ownership or licensing error is discovered.

The team must independently check provenance and compatibility; these assurances do not eliminate third-party claims.

## 5. Limitations and responsibility

Contributions are provided without extra warranties of security, quality, availability, maintenance, support, or fitness except where legally required or separately agreed. Open-source licensing and this agreement cannot exclude non-waivable statutory liability, prevent lawsuits, guarantee anonymity, or create a limited-liability company.

## 6. How to accept once for the organization

Before their first contribution under this policy, the contributor must post an **explicit acceptance** using an attributable GitHub account in the designated adoption issue or a pull request against `OpenDKG/.github`. Maintainers record the **version and exact commit SHA of this agreement**, account handle, timestamp, acceptance URL, and any specifically approved earlier contributions. The contributor should retain their own copy. Prefer verified GitHub/SSH/GPG signatures where practical; a key signature proves key control, not legal identity.

**Acceptance statement (copy and complete with actual values):**

> I, the GitHub account signing this comment, affirmatively accept **OpenDKG Contributor Agreement v1.0**, at the exact revision linked in this comment, for my authorized contributions to **all current and future Covered Repositories owned by OpenDKG**, excluding **NetPetra**. I understand that valid outbound licenses I grant on accepted contributions cannot be revoked solely because I leave the team. I retain ownership of my original work. My previously submitted work is included **only** if I list the relevant commits or PRs here. Covered earlier work: **none / links**. Agreement revision (commit SHA): **[paste commit]**.

A DCO `Signed-off-by` trailer is **not** acceptance of this separate agreement. Acceptance of a materially revised agreement requires a new explicit acknowledgment; changed terms cannot retroactively replace prior public licenses. The initial three maintainers must each individually accept this agreement and record adoption of `GOVERNANCE.md` before they claim these rules are in force.

## 7. Previous contributions

For code or assets created before acceptance, **obtain a separate, explicit identification and grant from each relevant rights-holder**. An acceptance must list existing commits, PRs, file paths or a sufficiently clear bounded range; writing "all our old code" without identifying the code and rights-holders is insufficient. Do not presume that adding a policy today licenses old unlicensed code. Existing lawful open-source licenses continue according to their own terms.

## 8. Contributor departure, termination and disputes

Departing developers retain copyright in their original works, and may lawfully reuse or license them elsewhere. The maintainers and public may continue to use previously **validly licensed** contributions subject to their license conditions. Departures do not authorize the deletion of proper copyright attribution or the cancellation of valid public licenses.

Reported disputes should be investigated with preserved evidence and without automated takedown assumptions; if rights are unclear, stop incorporating contested material pending review, remove or independently replace it when necessary, and seek legal advice. No governing jurisdiction or exclusive court is designated by this draft: applicable law and enforceability depend on the people and facts involved. Do not put private identity documents in public GitHub issues.
