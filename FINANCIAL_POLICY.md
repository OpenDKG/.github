# OpenDKG Financial Policy — v1.1 Draft

**Status: proposed only. No automatic authority to collect money, create bank/wallet accounts, or distribute funds.** This policy must be adopted by the three founders, implemented through a valid legal/payment structure, and supplemented by a separate [founders' economic agreement](FOUNDERS_ECONOMIC_AGREEMENT_DRAFT.md).

## Intended 1/3 / 1/3 / 1/3 rule

For **each eligible official OpenDKG project**, the three original founders are intended to participate equally: **exactly one-third of lawful, unrestricted distributable net proceeds each**. Equal shares apply regardless of Team Lead title, code quantity, recruited contributors or publication date, subject to valid agreement and existing third-party rights.

**Distributable net proceeds** = cleared eligible project receipts **minus** processor charges, refunds/chargebacks, legally payable taxes or withholding attributable to the receipts, approved actual project expenses, outstanding liabilities and agreed reserves. Individual founders remain responsible for their own personal tax obligations unless a legally valid agreement expressly allocates a different obligation. No founder may characterize an amount as distributable until grant restrictions, local law and financial obligations permit it.

### Classification matters

| Source | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Unrestricted project revenue or a genuinely unrestricted donation | May become distributable net proceeds after deductions, required legal checks and approval |
| Purpose-restricted donation or grant | **Held and used solely for the permitted purpose**; not split equally as personal income merely because it was received |
| Contract for work / service grant | Pay authorized performers and suppliers under the funding contract and valid agreements; only legally unrestricted residuals, if any, can enter the founders' distribution calculation |
| General OpenDKG donation | Allocate according to an approved organizational budget and donor terms; do not assume it is income of a specific project |
| Pre-existing funds from a project admitted later | Keep existing recipient obligations and ownership intact unless parties explicitly authorize changes |

**Example (hypothetical):** If EUR 900 is cleared unrestricted net proceeds *after* authorized costs, liabilities, fees and obligations, each founder's intended share is EUR 300. If EUR 900 is a restricted grant earmarked for infrastructure, the distributable amount may be EUR 0.

## Approvals and controls

1. Keep a per-project transaction ledger with amount/currency, donor restrictions, beneficiary, real recipient, receipt reference, refunds, expenses, reserves, tax treatment, purpose and two-person approval record.
2. The person or legal entity actually receiving funds is identified **before** public fundraising. GitHub organization membership is not a bank account or legally recognized recipient.
3. Two founders approve ordinary eligible project expenses/distributions in writing; no recipient alone approves a transfer to themselves. Conflict-of-interest cases require disclosure, an independent approver where possible, and additional legal safeguards.
4. All three founders must consent to changing their equal economic entitlement or admitting an exception. A 2-of-3 vote cannot sign for the dissenting founder or override restricted grant conditions.
5. Publish appropriate non-sensitive periodic income/expense summaries. Do **not** publish personal addresses, tax IDs, identity documents, account credentials, wallet recovery phrases or private donor information.
6. No cash payouts until the financial/legal structure, tax treatment, eligibility, signatures, applicable jurisdiction and payment/AML obligations have been verified.

## Important legal boundary

A nonprofit association is **not** automatically a suitable vehicle for distributing one-third profits to its founders. For example, Serbian association law restricts distribution of an association's assets and profits to founders/members, while permitting certain legitimate remuneration and expenses. A suitable cross-border contractual/entity arrangement needs review before implementation. This proposed policy does **not** create an LLC, partnership shield, bank authority or enforceable payment obligation by itself.

Sources for legal review: [Serbian Law on Associations](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_udruzenjima.html) (especially Articles 37 and 41); consult qualified advisers in the founders' relevant jurisdictions.
