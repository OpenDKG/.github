# OpenDKG Brand and Intellectual Property — v1.1 Draft

**Status: proposed, not yet adopted.**

- **Software licenses:** each repo has its own actual outbound license; Apache-2.0 is the intended default when legally available. A shared organizational policy does not silently license historic third-party code.
- **Creator copyright:** original authors retain legal ownership subject to their valid grants. Neither the three founders' equal economic share nor a GitHub Organization Owner role is an automatic copyright assignment.
- **Shared brand:** use of `OpenDKG`, logo, domains, donation pages and product names is subject to a named rights-holder, documented permission and Council-approved public branding. A GitHub account does not prove legal trademark title.
- **Official projects:** project admission documents ownership/provenance and permission to represent a project as part of the initiative. Existing individual projects cannot be transferred or relicensed by a 2-of-3 vote alone.
- **Contributors:** external contributors grant only the rights clearly covered by a valid license/CLA or specific written agreement. No automatic three-way assignment of their work.
- **Patents and standard implementations:** an Apache-2.0 software license is not a complete substitute for an explicit standards IPR/patent disclosure and implementation policy. Any claimed official standard or patent commitments require independent review.
- **Economic rights:** the founders' proposed equal one-third shares in eligible net proceeds are separate from copyright, patents, trademarks, licenses, and third-party rights. See the founders' separate draft agreement.
- **Exit:** leaving an organization does not retroactively rescind valid public licenses or change ownership of personally controlled assets. Transfers and buyouts require individual agreements.

Before any public claim of exclusive brand or ownership rights, verify whose rights they actually are. Do not imply endorsement by OASIS, OriginTrail, Umanitek, or any other unaffiliated entity.
