# OpenDKG Team Roles — v1.1 Draft

**Status: proposed, not yet adopted.** OpenDKG has **no Technical Steering Committee (TSC)**. The three original founders have equal standing, each with one Council vote and a proposed one-third economic share in every eligible OpenDKG project.

## Leadership model

| Function | Lead responsibility | Limits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineering & Security | Architecture, coding standards, integrations, security, interoperability, release coordination and technical drafts | Cannot unilaterally publish adopted specifications, override required reviews, or take another author's IP |
| Marketing & Partnerships | Website, campaigns, external communications, partner relationships and grant prospecting | Cannot sign contracts, promise governance seats, or allocate project revenue without documented authorization |
| Community & Contributor Operations | Recruiting, membership intake, contributor experience, community moderation, documentation and process quality | Cannot independently create founders, grant privileged GitHub access, or alter legal/economic rights |

The three founders assign one named founder to each function **only after a recorded 2-of-3 decision**. Do not guess a mapping from usernames to functions. Assignments can be reviewed or rotated with the same approval. All founders may work in every area; a Lead is accountable for coordination, not the exclusive performer.

## Decision boundaries

- Routine tasks: the responsible Lead coordinates within an approved scope and budget.
- Source-code changes: follow independent review and security controls in GOVERNANCE.md.
- Public official specification: follow SPECIFICATION_PROCESS.md; Council approval is required.
- Major policy, grant/contract, money, ownership and publishing commitments: Council approval and explicit signatures from actual contracting or rights-owning parties as applicable.
- Protected founder one-third economic participation: unanimous assent of the affected founders for a change; no delegation to Leads.

Meeting notes, role assignments and significant decisions should be recorded in issues or PRs, without exposing private identities, banking details or secrets.
