# Official OpenDKG Project Admission — v1.1 Draft

**Status: proposed, not yet adopted.**

## Scope and eligibility

An OpenDKG project is (a) a new repository created and published by the OpenDKG organization and registered in the project roster, or (b) an external or existing project **explicitly admitted** after documented approval and rights review. Merely being mentioned in a README, integrated with OpenDKG, or being maintained by a founder does **not** transfer ownership or create financial entitlements.

Every admitted official project is intended to participate in the founders' **1/3 / 1/3 / 1/3 eligible net proceeds model**. No exception may be made without all three founders' express approval and any relevant rights-holder authorization.

## Admission checklist

1. Open a public proposal describing purpose, users, source repository, technical lead contact, security implications and third-party dependencies.
2. Identify actual code, documentation, model, dataset, artwork, trademark and domain rightsholders. For existing work, document licenses, historic contributors and written permissions. **No retroactive copyright assignment by organization vote.**
3. Confirm a license for every repository (Apache-2.0 default when lawful), acknowledgments, CLA coverage, vulnerability reporting and release responsibility.
4. Record whether donation or grant funds already exist, who holds them, any restrictions, tax obligations and existing contracts. Existing restricted funds cannot be silently added to a common pool.
5. Council approves admission by 2 of 3 founders; record issue/PR, date, approvals and repository identity. No TSC or company sponsorship is required.
6. Add the project to the public roster and assign operational coordination to the existing functional Leads. Each project may have maintainers, but this does not create a new governance committee.
7. Before money is collected or distributed, obtain legally effective founder financial agreements and recipient/processor compliance.

For a **personal or third-party project**, a project-specific signed admission may be needed to specify licensing, branding, revenue eligibility, and contracts. External projects are out of scope until then.

## Removal or departure

Archiving a repository does not cancel existing licenses, donor obligations, accrued payments or other people's IP. A founder's already vested economic interest cannot be confiscated by a 2-of-3 governance vote; resolve exit and buyout separately, in writing and under applicable law.
