# OpenDKG Founders' Equal Economic Participation — Agreement Draft

**STATUS: UNSIGNED DRAFT. NOT EFFECTIVE BY UPLOADING, MERGING OR CLOSING A GITHUB ISSUE. LEGAL REVIEW AND EXPLICIT INDIVIDUAL ACCEPTANCE REQUIRED.**

## Parties and scope

The contemplated parties are the natural persons behind the three original founder accounts: `@a-51-50`, `@brxtrac`, and `@dreddster`. This public draft is **not a verified civil-identity contract**. Any enforceable executed agreement must privately identify the parties as applicable by law, specify governing law, dispute venue, execution date, enforceable signatures and the effective date. Do not publish passports, addresses, tax IDs or bank details.

This agreement is intended to cover official OpenDKG projects described in [PROJECT_ADMISSION.md](PROJECT_ADMISSION.md): projects first created and released under OpenDKG and existing projects specifically admitted after a provenance and rights review. An unaffiliated founder's personal repo does not automatically become common property.

## Equal economic participation

Subject to a legally effective execution, each founder receives an **equal, undivided contractual entitlement of one-third (1/3)** of the eligible distributable **net proceeds** of **each** covered project. No founder has a larger share because they originated an idea, code more, work on a certain project, manage a Team Lead function, or control credentials. All three are expected to actively contribute across the initiative, but a different contribution volume does not, by itself, change the ratio.

The meaning of eligible net proceeds, source restrictions, taxes, reserves and approvals is specified in [FINANCIAL_POLICY.md](FINANCIAL_POLICY.md); restricted grant or donation money does not become freely distributable merely by being received.

## Ownership and intellectual property

Equal **economic** participation is not automatically joint copyright ownership, an assignment of source-code ownership, a transfer of trademarks, or a transfer of personal domain/account ownership. Existing open-source grants remain in force. If the founders also desire equal legal title to any particular copyright, registered trademark, domain or other asset, obtain a **separate express rights-holder assignment or co-ownership instrument** consistent with third-party rights and applicable law. No founder can transfer what they do not own.
